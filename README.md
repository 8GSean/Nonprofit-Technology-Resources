# Nonprofit Technology Resources

A curated collection of programs that provide meaningful value to nonprofits,
charities, educational organizations, and community groups.

## Best value at a glance

These are the offers with the clearest and largest savings. Prices and
eligibility can vary by country, plan, and organization size.

| Service | Nonprofit price or discount | Best use |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Google Workspace | **Free** for the nonprofit edition; paid upgrades from about **$3.50/user/month** | Email, documents, storage, meetings |
| Microsoft 365 | **Free** Business Basic for up to 300 users; paid plans up to about **75% off** | Office, email, Teams, device management |
| Canva | **100% free** for eligible teams of up to 50 users | Design, campaigns, social media, presentations |
| Slack | **100% free** Pro for workspaces up to 250 members; **85% off** above 250 members and on Business+ | Team communication and volunteer coordination |
| Claude for Nonprofits | Up to **75% off** eligible Team and Enterprise plans | Research, writing, analysis, and AI assistance |
| GitHub | **Free** Team plan; **25% off** Enterprise Cloud | Code, websites, automation, and collaboration |
| Cloudflare Workers and Pages | **$0** free plans; no nonprofit approval required | Websites, documentation, deployments, and lightweight APIs |
| Salesforce | **10 free donated licenses** through Power of Us | Donor, constituent, fundraising, and volunteer CRM |
| Zoom | **50% off** select products for eligible nonprofits | Video meetings and webinars |

Start with the free offers before applying for smaller discounts. A discount
is only useful if the product fits the organization's workflow and the
remaining cost is affordable.

## Google for Nonprofits

Google Workspace for Nonprofits is **free** for eligible organizations,
normally up to 300 users initially. It includes custom-domain email, Drive,
Docs, Sheets, Slides, Calendar, and Meet. Google may allow larger
organizations to request a higher limit.

Paid upgrades are discounted; Business Standard is listed at approximately
**$3.50/user/month** on an annual commitment, with larger enterprise plans
discounted substantially.

https://www.google.com/nonprofits/

## Microsoft for Nonprofits

Eligible nonprofits can receive Microsoft 365 Business Basic **free for up to
300 users**. It includes web and mobile Office apps, Outlook, Teams,
Exchange, SharePoint, and 1 TB of OneDrive storage per user.

Paid nonprofit plans, such as Business Standard and Business Premium, are
available at nonprofit pricing, commonly up to approximately **75% below
commercial pricing**. Check the Microsoft portal for the organization's
country and current rates.

https://www.microsoft.com/nonprofits

## Canva for Nonprofits

Canva Pro is **100% free** for eligible nonprofits and teams of up to 50
users. It includes premium design tools, brand management, templates, and
collaboration features. Canva also advertises discounted additional seats or
enterprise options for larger teams.

https://www.canva.com/nonprofits/

## Slack for Nonprofits

Slack offers one of the clearest nonprofit discounts:

* **100% off:** Slack Pro is free for eligible workspaces with up to 250
  members.
* **85% off:** Pro for larger workspaces and Business+ for eligible
  organizations.

This removes the normal free-plan limitations that are especially painful for
nonprofits, including message history limits.

https://slack.com/help/articles/204368833-Apply-for-the-Slack-for-Nonprofits-discount

## Claude for Nonprofits

Anthropic's Claude for Nonprofits program offers up to **75% off** eligible
Team and Enterprise plans for qualifying organizations. The discount does not
apply to individual Claude Pro accounts, and the final price depends on the
plan and billing arrangement.

Claude is particularly useful for research, grant writing, analysis,
communications, coding, and creating internal knowledge tools.

https://www.anthropic.com/news/claude-for-nonprofits

## GitHub for Nonprofits

Eligible nonprofits can receive:

* **Free:** GitHub Team for nonprofits.
* **25% off:** GitHub Enterprise Cloud.

This is useful for technical nonprofits, makerspaces, STEM education,
open-source projects, websites, and automation. GitHub Team includes private
repositories and advanced collaboration features.

https://github.com/solutions/industry/nonprofits

## Cloudflare Workers and Pages

Cloudflare does not need a nonprofit discount to be useful:

* **$0/month:** Pages free plan for static websites and deployments.
* **$0/month:** Workers free plan for lightweight serverless code, subject to
  current usage limits.
* **From $5/month:** Workers Paid for workloads that exceed the free limits.

Pages is a strong fit for nonprofit websites, documentation, landing pages,
and public resources. Workers can add APIs, forms, authentication, and other
lightweight dynamic features. Check the current limits before relying on the
free plan for production workloads.

https://www.cloudflare.com/plans/developer-platform/

## Salesforce for Nonprofits

Salesforce's Power of Us Program provides eligible nonprofits with **10 free
donated Salesforce licenses**. Additional licenses and products may have
nonprofit pricing, but implementation, data migration, and administration are
not free.

Salesforce can be valuable for donor, constituent, fundraising, grant, and
volunteer management, but it usually requires more setup and specialist
administration than the tools above.

https://www.salesforce.org/power-of-us/

## Zoom for Nonprofits

Eligible nonprofits can receive **50% off** selected Zoom products, including
Zoom Workplace offerings. Verification is handled through Goodstack, and
eligible products can vary by region and organization.

https://www.zoom.com/en/zoom-cares/

## AWS Nonprofit Credits

AWS nonprofit credits are not a percentage discount, but they can offset cloud
costs:

* Common TechSoup offer: **$1,000 in AWS credits per year** for smaller
  eligible nonprofits.
* Larger organizations may qualify for **$2,000 or $5,000** in annual
  credits.
* TechSoup may charge an administration fee, and credits expire and have
  service restrictions.

Use AWS credits for hosting, storage, databases, and serverless workloads only
with active budget and usage monitoring.

https://aws.amazon.com/government-education/nonprofits/nonprofit-credit-program/

## TechSoup

TechSoup is a primary gateway to nonprofit technology programs and validation.
It provides access to offers from Microsoft, Adobe, AWS, and other vendors.
Some offers include administration fees, so compare the final cost rather than
assuming every catalog item is free.

https://www.techsoup.org/

Israel:
https://www.techsoupisrael.org.il/

## Goodstack

Goodstack is a verification platform used by several nonprofit programs,
including selected Google, Microsoft, Zoom, and Anthropic offers.

Goodstack itself is not the benefit; the value comes from the partner program
that it unlocks.

https://goodstack.org/

## Tov-learn

An open-source learning platform for technical education.

Includes:

- Interactive AI-guided lessons
- Spaced repetition and quizzes
- Progress tracking and dashboards
- Project-based learning workflows

https://github.com/TovTechOrg/Tov-learn
